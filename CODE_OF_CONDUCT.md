# Code of Conduct

smart-panel is a small project for people who like making hardware do things it wasn't designed
to do. We want it to be a friendly place to learn, ask questions, and share work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Be welcoming to newcomers, including people who are new to USB, Python, or reverse engineering.
- Give and accept feedback constructively. Reviews are about the code, not the contributor.
- Keep discussion focused on the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or behavior based on race, ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation,
  disability, age, religion, nationality, or any similar characteristic.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission. This includes
  posting USB captures or logs that contain other people's data.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct a reasonable person would consider
  inappropriate in a collaborative setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests, and discussions, and
to any other space where someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They may edit
or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they judge inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, Cameron Horton ([@hortocam](https://github.com/hortocam)),
through GitHub. Reports will be handled promptly and with discretion.
